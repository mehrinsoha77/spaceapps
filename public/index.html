    Does the dose engine match a real spacecraft?

    NASA's Curiosity rover measured radiation inside its spacecraft on the way to Mars (MSL/RAD). Three storms hit during that cruise. SHELTER estimates the same doses from GOES data at Earth and the published shielding around the detector, then compares.
